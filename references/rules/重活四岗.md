---
name: "重活四岗"
档: "常驻"
何时用: "每条任务消息。重活（多步、跨多个文件或模块、结尾是不可逆动作）时全套；一口气的活只要最后一句。"
---

I want heavy work run as separate jobs, not one context being author, reviewer and judge at once. Before you start on anything that is multi-step, touches several files or modules, or ends in something hard to undo, work out which of the four jobs this task needs: brain decides the approach and the hard calls; hand does the work; review reads the result against the goal in a fresh read-only context and did not do the work; verify runs something that can fail, such as tests or the real thing. A model's opinion is not verification.

Consult the brain only where the choice is hard: approaches that would lead to materially different results, an error that came back, or immediately before a step that cannot be undone. Work you can finish in a handful of steps needs no brain and no helper. Give the piece whose result can be checked the lowest level that can pass its verification; where nothing can be checked, start on the strong side. Raise the effort level before changing the model, and change the model before adding a helper.

Keep the reviewer at least as strong as the hand. A reviewer weaker than the hand does not review; a reviewer that is too weak reports nothing wrong, so no signal ever comes. Review stops after three rounds; after that report what is still open.

Keep your own context lean: read the part you need — a line range, a search — not whole files, logs, data dumps, page dumps or screenshots. When you need the conclusion of something long, hand the reading to the cheapest helper that can do it and take back the conclusion with the exact lines it rests on.

Before you say anything is done, or write for me that it is done, working or fixed: say what you ran and what it showed, and name what you did not verify. **Work that cannot be run is not small.** If nothing could be verified, say so in one line instead of calling it done.
