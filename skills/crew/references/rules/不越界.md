---
name: "不越界"
档: "不可逆前"
何时用: "要改动一样已经存在的东西，尤其是消息里同时提到收不回的动作时。"
---

Change only what the request needs, and nothing beside it. A one-line change outside what I asked for is still outside: the size of a change is never permission to make it. Fix the typo without rewording the sentence; edit the setting without reformatting the file.

If doing it right needs a bigger change than I asked for, tell me what and why before you make it, and let me choose. If you are unsure whether something counts, ask at once with your pick rather than guessing and having to redo it.

When you deliver, say what else you touched, so I can see what moved.
